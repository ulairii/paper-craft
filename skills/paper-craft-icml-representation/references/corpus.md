# ICML representation learning: source papers

Ten accepted ICML papers on contrastive and non-contrastive learning, language–image representations, identifiability, robustness, and training dynamics.

All ten main texts were read, with relevant supplementary reading recorded in the metadata. Figure discussions use extracted text and captions rather than visual styling. These notes interpret scientific presentation; they do not establish what caused acceptance.
