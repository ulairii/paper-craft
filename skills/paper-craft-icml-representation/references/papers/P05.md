# P05: Understanding self-supervised learning dynamics without contrastive pairs

Tian, Yuandong; Chen, Xinlei; Ganguli, Surya. **ICML 2021**. [Official paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/tian21a.html) · [Full text](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/tian21a/tian21a.pdf).

Source locations: Sections 2–4; Theorems 1–3; Tables 1, 4 and 9; Figures 2, 5–6; supplementary Sections A, B and D.1–D.3.

## Writing move: Derive an algorithm from a training-dynamics explanation

DirectPred begins with the puzzle of why non-contrastive learning avoids collapse. A simplified model yields predictions about the interaction of the predictor, weight decay, and target updates. The proposed algorithm arrives after the explanation, so it feels like a consequence of an insight rather than an unrelated engineering addition.

## Experimental logic

The paper first studies dynamics and then asks whether their qualitative predictions appear in deep networks. The failure of substituting an instantaneous least-squares predictor motivates studying the optimization path rather than only fixed points. Comparisons with linear and nonlinear learned predictors make clear what the derived update replaces.

## Detail placement

Put the model assumptions beside the claims they enable: linear networks, gradient-flow dynamics, and the additional simplifying conditions used in particular results. Explain the predictor's relation to feature second moments in the method; leave detailed eigenvalue derivations in supporting material.

## Interpretation

Qualitative agreement in nonlinear networks is evidence that the model is informative, not a proof that its assumptions hold there. The reported gain over a linear predictor should not become a gain over every BYOL variant. Optimization-free refers to the predictor update, not to training the encoder; hybrid update variants also need their own description.
