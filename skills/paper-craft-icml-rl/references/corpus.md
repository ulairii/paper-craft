# ICML reinforcement learning: source papers

Ten accepted ICML papers on policy optimization, asynchronous learning, distributional values, actor–critic estimation, offline learning, visual control, replay, planning, and sequence-policy adaptation.

All ten main texts were read. Supplementary reading is described in the source metadata. Figure discussions use extracted text and captions rather than visual styling. The lessons interpret persuasive scientific presentation; they do not establish what caused acceptance.
