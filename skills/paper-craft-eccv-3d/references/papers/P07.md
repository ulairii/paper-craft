## Writing move: Use a shared coordinate system to explain fusion

BEVFormer gives cameras and time a common spatial reference. Grid queries correspond to physical places; spatial attention gathers projected image evidence, and temporal attention incorporates aligned history. This ordering lets readers understand the fusion problem before encountering the attention modules.

## Experimental logic

A static variant separates spatial aggregation from temporal information. Velocity and low-visibility results explain why history helps beyond aggregate detection scores. Joint detection and segmentation expose mixed task effects, which refine the unification claim rather than invalidate it.

## Detail placement

Keep calibration, ego-motion alignment, and past-only access explicit. History is detached during the described training procedure. Separate encoder latency from the much larger image-backbone and full-system cost. Waymo's visible-region, vehicle-only evaluation belongs with any cross-dataset claim.

## Interpretation

Avoid translating 'no explicit depth prediction' into 'no geometric calibration.' Temporal gains need not improve size and attributes equally. The calibration-noise comparison supports robustness under the tested perturbations, not independence from camera parameters.
