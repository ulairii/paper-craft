# P05: SSN: Shape Signature Networks for Multi-class Object Detection from Point Clouds

Xinge Zhu      Yuexin Ma      Tai Wang      Yan Xu Jianping Shi      Dahua Lin. **ECCV 2020**. [Official paper](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2020/papers_ECCV/html/5131_ECCV_2020_paper.php) · [Full text](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2020/papers_ECCV/papers/123700579.pdf).

Source locations: Sections 3.2–3.4 and 4.3–4.4; Tables 3–5; Figures 2 and 4.

## Writing move: Give an auxiliary target a geometric meaning

SSN motivates shape signatures through categories whose sparse observations can be difficult to separate. Canonicalized object crops become projected shape summaries and compact coefficients. Explaining that chain gives the auxiliary target a meaning that an unexplained nine-dimensional vector would lack.

## Experimental logic

Incremental head and signature comparisons separate the full detector from the training target. Adding signatures to different backbones tests portability. Comparing shape-based groups with one class per head asks whether grouping has value beyond simply adding prediction branches.

## Detail placement

State that the auxiliary regression head is removed at inference, while grouped detection heads remain. Preserve the category-mean fallback for very sparse crops and the fact that scale is retained. Put coefficient-generation mechanics after the geometric intuition.

## Interpretation

A convex-hull summary is a useful shape prior, not recovery of the complete surface. Without separate ablations, symmetry completion and fitting are motivated design choices rather than individually proven necessities. Do not interpret different dataset metric scales as direct evidence of a domain gap.
