## Writing move: Explain sparsity through the missing prediction location

SWFormer connects sparse attention to an operational problem: occupied surface voxels need to support predictions at potentially empty object centers. Window hierarchy expands context, while foreground-guided voxel diffusion activates additional locations. Each mechanism answers a different part of that spatial difficulty.

## Experimental logic

Diffusion range tests whether activating nearby locations helps, with gains that plateau rather than increase indefinitely. Multi-scale comparisons and large-vehicle, high-overlap results connect receptive field to localization needs. These targeted results explain the design better than a table of undifferentiated module gains.

## Detail placement

Define diffusion here as occupancy expansion followed by feature processing, not a generative diffusion model. New locations begin with zero features. Explain occupancy bucketing and sparse upsampling at a high level; implementation detail matters mainly when it explains actual runtime.

## Interpretation

The reported 20 ms timing uses a specified frame and optimized implementation, not a dataset-wide latency average. Separate single-frame and multi-frame results, and name the evaluated classes. Do not turn a favorable large-object trend into an all-category claim.
