## Writing move: Separate a better pretext score from a better representation

Point-MAE explains why masked learning needs adaptation to point clouds: irregular patches and their positional information differ from image patches. Delaying mask tokens until the decoder addresses what information reaches the encoder. The resulting architecture is easier to understand when introduced as an information-flow decision.

## Experimental logic

The instructive comparison is that lower reconstruction loss can accompany worse transfer. Mask ratio, token placement, and model size therefore test representation quality through downstream performance rather than treating reconstruction error as the final goal. Voting and increased input point counts remain separate result settings.

## Detail placement

Explain patch formation, local coordinates, and where mask positions enter. The backbone uses standard transformers but patch embedding still uses a point network. For segmentation, describe the feature upsampling used to predict all points rather than generalizing a classification simplification to every task.

## Interpretation

Delaying position information does not remove all geometric overlap or all decoder access to position. CAD pretraining followed by real-scan fine-tuning demonstrates transfer with adaptation, not zero-shot scene understanding. Treat the observed masking range as an empirical choice.
