## Writing move: Put the desired invariance in the representation

PPF-FoldNet connects rotation sensitivity to the descriptor's input. Distances and angles between oriented point pairs remove global orientation before learning. Reconstructing this representation gives the unsupervised objective a clear purpose: preserve local geometric relationships that remain useful after rotation.

## Experimental logic

Rotated 3DMatch directly addresses the motivation. Density reduction tests the same trained descriptor under fewer observations. The comparison among point-pair feature variants is useful even though the chosen variant is not the strongest in every row; simplicity can remain part of the contribution.

## Detail placement

Explain that the network reconstructs point-pair features, not the original point coordinates. Surface normals are still needed to construct the input. Keep the descriptor sampling count and the definition of feature-matching recall near the relevant result; this recall is not final registration success.

## Interpretation

Rotation invariance does not imply scale invariance or immunity to noisy normals. Do not describe the two-point geometric proposition as unique reconstruction of an entire patch. Compare runtime using the full preprocessing-plus-network measurement rather than only the network component.
