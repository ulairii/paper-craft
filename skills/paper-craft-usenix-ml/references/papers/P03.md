# P03: Turning Your Weakness Into a Strength: Watermarking Deep Neural Networks by Backdooring

Yossi Adi; Carsten Baum; Moustapha Cisse; Benny Pinkas; Joseph Keshet. **USENIX Security 2018**. [Official paper](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity18/presentation/adi) · [Full text](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/conference/usenixsecurity18/sec18-adi.pdf).

Source locations: Sections 1–7; watermark construction, removal experiments and transfer tests; Appendix A.

## Writing move: Let desired properties organize a method borrowed from an adversary

Turning Your Weakness Into a Strength repurposes backdoor behavior as a private ownership marker. The conceptual reversal makes the contribution memorable without requiring ornate language. Desired properties—ordinary accuracy, verification, persistence, and resistance to competing claims—then organize the design and experiments. Commitment explains why possessing a trigger set after the fact is insufficient.

## Experimental logic

Training from scratch and adding a marker to a pretrained model receive separate treatment because their persistence differs. Fine-tuning, output-layer reinitialization, and adding another marker probe different removal strategies. Transfer experiments ask whether a marker survives changes to the task, while ordinary accuracy establishes the cost of embedding it.

## Detail placement

Keep when the marker is inserted, who can query it, and which model layer is replaced or restored in the main text. The transfer test restores the original output layer for verification; that detail defines the demonstrated capability. Cryptographic definitions and the proposed public verification extension can follow the practical private-verification story.

## Interpretation

The formal construction relies on its assumed backdoor properties; empirical persistence does not prove that every marker is unremovable. The public zero-knowledge proposal is distinct from the evaluated private protocol. Verification evidence does not by itself establish legal ownership. A useful draft explains the particular persistence obtained under the tested transformations.
