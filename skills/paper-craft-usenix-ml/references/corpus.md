# USENIX Security machine learning security: source papers

Ten accepted USENIX Security papers on adversarial inputs, poisoning, privacy inference, watermarking, certified defense, incident response, and comparative risk measurement.

All ten main texts and their relevant published appendices were read. Exact coverage is recorded below in the metadata; separately cited technical reports are not counted as read. Most PDFs include a cover page; Local Model Poisoning begins on PDF page 1. Figure discussion uses text and captions rather than visual-style analysis. These notes interpret writing craft in accepted papers, not the causes of acceptance.
