# ICCV object detection: source papers

Ten accepted ICCV papers spanning shared detector computation, geometric alignment, loss design, anchor-free prediction, task alignment, pseudo labels, and transformer training. Mask R-CNN is included for its detector-alignment and joint-training lessons; its primary task is instance segmentation.

All ten main texts and both pages of the Stable Matching supplement were read. Other supplements are not the basis of these notes. Figure discussions use extracted text and captions rather than visual styling. The lessons interpret persuasive scientific presentation; they do not establish what caused acceptance.
