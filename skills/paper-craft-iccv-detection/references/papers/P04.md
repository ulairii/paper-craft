## Writing move: Let one diagnosed training problem carry the paper

Focal Loss asks why dense one-stage detectors trail proposal-based systems, then points to the aggregate influence of easy background examples. The loss is introduced as a response to that imbalance. RetinaNet provides the vehicle for demonstrating the principle, while inherited architectural ideas are credited openly.

## Experimental logic

Initialization and class-balanced cross entropy establish a functioning baseline before the focal term is tested. The cumulative loss distributions show how the term changes the contribution of negatives. OHEM comparisons distinguish continuous downweighting from selecting a subset of examples.

## Detail placement

Define the modulating factor through its behavior on easy and difficult examples before discussing the focusing parameter. Retain the prior initialization because it also stabilizes the baseline. Keep backbone and input-scale improvements separate from the loss contribution.

## Interpretation

The distribution analysis changes the loss exponent on predictions from one converged model; it is not a set of independently trained causal interventions. Focal loss is not a robust loss that suppresses outliers: it emphasizes difficult examples. The paper leaves the highest-frame-rate regime outside scope.
