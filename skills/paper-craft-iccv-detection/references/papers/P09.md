## Writing move: Connect denser supervision to the part that receives it

Co-DETR separates sparse supervision of encoder features from inefficient learning of positive decoder queries. Auxiliary one-to-many heads address the first; positive coordinates extracted from them address the second. This division gives the collaborative training scheme an information-flow explanation.

## Experimental logic

Feature and attention diagnostics support the proposed learning interpretation. Components are tested separately, and longer-schedule baselines ask whether simply training more achieves the same result. Varying auxiliary heads shows that adding more diverse supervision eventually stops helping.

## Detail placement

Clearly mark training-only heads and explain which queries reach the shared decoder. Distinguish epochs from training cost: the paper reports GPU hours as well as memory. Place larger-data and larger-backbone results after the core comparisons.

## Interpretation

Feature norms and activation-map distances are diagnostic proxies, not direct measurements of semantic discriminability or gradient conflict. The core scheme can retain NMS-free inference, but §4.3 adds NMS in stronger Co-DINO configurations. State the configuration-specific claim instead of copying the abstract indiscriminately.
