## Writing move: Give an adaptive operation a concrete contrast

Deformable ConvNets contrast fixed sampling grids with learned, input-dependent offsets. A single change explains both deformable convolution and deformable RoI pooling. The reader sees what moves and what remains shared before encountering the implementation equations.

## Experimental logic

Comparisons with fixed dilation ask whether a larger receptive field alone explains the improvement. Effective dilation is measured across object sizes, connecting the learned offsets to the motivating geometric variation. Tests in several detector and segmentation frameworks support portability.

## Detail placement

Distinguish per-image, per-location offsets from static learned parameters. Bilinear interpolation explains how fractional samples are evaluated and trained. Keep these details; summarize routine network adaptations once.

## Interpretation

The effective-dilation statistic is a rough measure of sampling extent, not proof of full transformation invariance. Additional layers eventually give little benefit. Small parameter overhead makes capacity alone less plausible as an explanation, but does not mathematically isolate the cause.
