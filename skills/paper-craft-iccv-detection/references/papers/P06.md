## Writing move: Make a missing observation visible before adding a branch

This CenterNet adds a center keypoint to CornerNet’s paired corners. The motivating failure is concrete: plausible corners can form a box whose interior does not support the predicted object. Center and cascade-corner pooling then explain how the additional evidence is obtained.

## Experimental logic

The error analysis returns to incorrect boxes, especially small ones. Component results assign different roles to central-region exploration and pooling. Replacing predicted center keypoints with ground truth reveals a remaining bottleneck; it is an oracle analysis, not deployable performance.

## Detail placement

Explain why a geometric center may lack a distinctive local appearance and how pooling reaches informative regions. Keep this keypoint-triplet CenterNet distinct from the separately named Objects as Points method. The central-region size rule belongs because it mediates precision and recall.

## Interpretation

The paper’s FD metric is defined from one minus AP at low overlap thresholds. Do not casually rewrite it as a literal raw fraction of false boxes. Its single-scale evaluation also includes horizontal flipping. Copy the explanatory idea, not every informal characterization of the evidence.
