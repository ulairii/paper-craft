## Writing move: Separate two kinds of label reliability

Soft Teacher explains why high-confidence pseudo labels create a second problem: they improve positive precision while leaving some true objects labeled as background. Teacher-based background weighting addresses that issue. Regression stability under box jitter addresses a different question, whether the pseudo box is accurately located.

## Experimental logic

The foreground precision–recall curve motivates the classification treatment. Score-versus-localization and variance-versus-localization plots motivate a separate regression filter. Experiments move from limited-label settings to strengthening an already well-trained detector, making the second regime a meaningful test of usefulness.

## Detail placement

Distinguish the teacher update, the student’s box candidates, and the two pseudo-box sets. The classification mechanism weights background losses; it is not simply a soft class vector assigned to every positive box. The training loop makes updated teacher information available continuously.

## Interpretation

When published supervised baselines differ, show each baseline and its gain rather than attributing the final-score difference entirely to the semi-supervised method. The chosen foreground threshold trades precision against recall; neither an arbitrarily strict threshold nor a high classification score guarantees good localization.
