## Writing move: Make simplification advance both practice and understanding

Fast R-CNN inventories concrete costs of the earlier pipeline: repeated feature extraction, separate training stages, and cached features. Shared computation and joint training directly answer this inventory. The final discussion adds a scientific payoff: faster experiments make previously expensive questions about proposals practical.

## Experimental logic

The joint-training comparison disables box regression at inference to expose the effect on classification before testing the full system. The proposal-count study finds that adding proposals can reduce AP even as a recall proxy increases. These are explanations of the detector, not just a faster runtime table.

## Detail placement

Hierarchical image/RoI sampling belongs in the main argument because it makes shared training possible. The pooling derivative also matters to the claim that earlier layers can be updated. State when reported detection time excludes proposal generation.

## Interpretation

A single-stage training procedure is not the same as a proposal-free one-stage detector. Softmax only slightly beats SVMs; that small difference is enough to support the simpler procedure without an inflated accuracy claim.
