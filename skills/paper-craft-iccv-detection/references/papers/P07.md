## Writing move: Define alignment through the decision it changes

TOOD explains task misalignment through an operational consequence: a high classification score can preserve a poorly localized box during NMS. Shared interactive features, task-specific prediction, and aligned assignment address this consequence at different parts of the learning process.

## Experimental logic

Head replacement across detectors tests architectural reuse. Assignment comparisons separate selecting positives from weighting them. Ranking agreement, top-scoring-box IoU, and the counts of retained boxes return to the stated decision problem rather than relying on AP alone.

## Detail placement

Explain what is aligned: classification confidence and localization quality at candidate locations. Retain the normalization of the alignment target because raw small values can undermine learning. Avoid burying the reader in intermediate feature dimensions before establishing this role.

## Interpretation

The combined head and learning scheme outperform either alone in the reported setting. That is a specific interaction, not a general law of task synergy. The alignment argument applies to the evaluated assignment regime; Stable Matching in this corpus shows why a superficially similar target can behave differently under one-to-one matching.
