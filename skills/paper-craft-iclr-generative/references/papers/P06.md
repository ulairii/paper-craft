# P06: Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations

Yang Song; Jascha Sohl-Dickstein; Diederik P Kingma; Abhishek Kumar; Stefano Ermon; Ben Poole. **ICLR 2021**. [Official paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=PxTIG12RRHS) · [Full text read: arXiv:2011.13456v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2).

Source locations: Sections 2–5; Tables 1–3; Appendices D, G–I.

## Writing move: Make unification earn its place through new capabilities

The score-SDE paper rewrites earlier approaches in common notation, then uses that connection to derive new sampling choices and conditional generation. The accessible noise-to-data explanation comes before stochastic calculus. Each capability gets its own reasoning and evidence, which prevents a broad framework paper from becoming a list of unrelated applications.

## Experimental logic

Table 1 compares predictor-corrector sampling with predictor-only alternatives at matched score-evaluation budgets. Tables 2 and 3 separate likelihood and sample quality, and the strongest results also include architectural and training changes. Conditional examples show additional uses of an unconditional score model; they do not replace the central generative evaluation.

## Detail placement

Keep the meaning of the score, the reverse process, and the practical consequence of each derivation in the main text. Put extended calculus, sampler grids, and architecture inventories in appendices. Explain that class conditioning requires an auxiliary noisy-image classifier, even when the score model is not retrained.

## Interpretation

The ODE likelihood formula is evaluated numerically with a trace estimator. Shared SDE/ODE marginals rely on the ideal score, and learned finite-step samplers can differ. Best likelihood and best FID come from different configurations; the tables also use different checkpoint and sampler choices. Present the observed capabilities without claiming one model simultaneously attains every headline result.
