## Writing move: Sell a useful combination of properties

Real NVP makes its contribution legible as a combination: tractable density evaluation, inverse inference, and parallel sample generation. The method then explains why ordinary transformations make determinants and inversion expensive, and how a triangular coupling avoids those obstacles. Alternating masks explains how variables left unchanged in one layer can still be transformed later. Each architectural choice follows from a required property.

## Experimental logic

Table 1 evaluates density modeling; samples, interpolation, and latent resampling examine different uses of the invertible representation. The table does not establish the best likelihood against every competitor. Its distinction between exact model likelihoods and reported variational bounds matters more than a sweeping rank claim. Preprocessing and its Jacobian explain what bits per dimension measures.

## Detail placement

Keep the coupling transformation, tractable determinant, inverse, and likelihood convention in the main account. Put repeated galleries and secondary latent manipulations in supporting material. Distinguish the label-conditioned attribute experiments in Appendix F from unconditional training.

## Interpretation

Exact sampling refers to the learned model, not equality to the true data distribution. The appendix describes predominantly graphical latent features rather than established high-level semantic disentanglement. A properties-based contribution can remain compelling when an autoregressive competitor has stronger likelihood.
