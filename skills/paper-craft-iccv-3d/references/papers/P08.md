## Writing move: Give receptive-field expansion a concrete spatial purpose

Voxel Transformer motivates larger support with a tangible mismatch: a sparse convolutional receptive field can be smaller than a car. The method distinguishes extending spatial reach from increasing the number of attended voxels. Local and dilated attention, and sparse versus submanifold operations, follow from that purpose.

## Experimental logic

Replacing backbones in two detector families demonstrates usefulness in different pipelines. Local/dilated and attended-voxel ablations explain the representation. Distance breakdowns connect expanded context to sparse far-away objects. Dropout can hurt severely in this setting; a familiar transformer recipe is not automatically appropriate for a small set of sparse observations.

## Detail placement

Keep the role of empty versus occupied query locations and the efficient query mechanism in the method. These define how attention operates on sparse voxels. Use a short architecture summary for the remaining layers. Distinguish the dataset split and metric convention rather than combining incompatible KITTI numbers.

## Interpretation

Fewer parameters coexist with lower inference rates than the replaced backbones. Sell the accuracy–context tradeoff directly, not as a speed improvement. A favorable overall result does not require every easy, moderate, and hard cell to improve.
