## Writing move: Change the unit of computation and explain its information cost

Superpoint Transformer compresses a scene into geometric regions before learning their relationships. Fast partitioning and hierarchical attention form one efficiency story: the model reasons over a smaller, meaningful representation. Handcrafted features are an explicit ingredient, not something to conceal behind end-to-end language.

## Experimental logic

Time-to-quality comparisons include preprocessing, making the proposed computational unit understandable as a full workflow. Hierarchy, adjacency, and partition-coarseness experiments separate compression from contextual learning. A majority-label partition oracle estimates the ceiling imposed by regions and shows a substantial remaining classification gap; it does not mean boundary information is recoverable after a bad partition.

## Detail placement

Explain how point features produce regions, how regions connect, and how labels are assigned at different hierarchy levels. State the finest prediction unit. Put the full descriptor inventory and partition algorithm in supplementary detail after identifying their roles in the main text.

## Interpretation

The same compression that saves computation can erase weak boundaries and make larger models overfit. The supplement explicitly discusses both. Distinguish dataset-specific model sizes and results: strong six-fold performance is not a claim to lead every split or dataset. The limitation is part of the representation's scientific meaning.
