# NeurIPS representation learning: source papers

Ten accepted NeurIPS papers on self-supervised learning, supervised contrastive objectives, graph representations, local features, label efficiency, and transfer analysis.

All ten main texts were read, along with the relevant supplements recorded in the metadata. Figure discussions use extracted text and captions rather than visual styling. These notes interpret scientific presentation; they do not establish what caused acceptance.
