## Writing move: Separate a learning objective from the efficiency of its implementation

SwAV replaces direct feature comparison with prediction of assignments across views. Explaining what is swapped gives the method a memorable identity. Online assignments address a practical obstacle in clustering methods: repeatedly processing the full dataset. Multicrop training is introduced as a second contribution with its own purpose.

## Experimental logic

The comparison with related clustering and contrastive objectives under common augmentation settings isolates the learning strategy. Multicrop comparisons then establish the value of additional local views. DeepCluster-v2 can be slightly stronger in accuracy, so online operation supplies a distinct reason to use SwAV. Fixed-prototype and assignment studies help explain which parts of clustering are actually needed.

## Detail placement

Explain why global views provide assignment targets and local views predict them. State when a queue supports small batches. Keep the chosen crop configuration beside cost claims: the supplement reports different time and memory costs for different multicrop recipes.

## Interpretation

Balanced assignments discourage degenerate solutions but do not make collapse impossible. Prototypes need not correspond one-to-one to semantic classes. Multicrop is not costless, and lower assignment loss need not mean better downstream features. The useful contribution is a combination of online learning and effective view use.
