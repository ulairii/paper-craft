## Writing move: Sell a useful tradeoff with measurements of both sides

VICRegL frames local and global feature learning as a tradeoff. Coordinate-based matching supplies spatial correspondence, while feature-based matching allows associations beyond the same image location. The method reuses the VICReg criterion and makes the balance between local and global learning an explicit, interpretable choice.

## Experimental logic

Classification and segmentation appear together because the central claim concerns both. Frozen linear readouts test whether useful information is readily accessible; fine-tuned results answer a different practical question. Matching ablations and experiments with and without multicrop explain the local criterion separately from the training recipe.

## Detail placement

Keep the feature layer, matching rule, selected-pair filtering, balance coefficient, and readout inputs in the main explanation. Distinguish short ConvNeXt ablations from longer final models and larger-data variants. Move complete schedules and additional matching illustrations to the appendix.

## Interpretation

The result preserves most classification performance while improving segmentation; it is not a costless improvement on every axis. The supplement reports additional time and memory. A grid location in a matching illustration is not the full receptive field, and neither the plot nor a feature-distance match proves object-level correspondence.
