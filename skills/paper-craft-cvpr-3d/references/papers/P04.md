## Writing move: Explain why a design transfers differently to 3D

PointRCNN motivates bottom-up proposals through a property of the 3D setting: box annotations can identify foreground points in the studied scenes. Canonical-coordinate refinement then reduces the variation the second stage must model. The sequence follows the information available in the data, not a generic two-stage recipe.

## Experimental logic

Proposal recall at different budgets explains the first stage. Canonical transformation and semantic-feature comparisons explain the second. Context-width experiments show that too little context omits useful evidence while too much includes neighboring objects. The loss comparison addresses the localization formulation.
