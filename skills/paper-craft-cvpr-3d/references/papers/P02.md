## Writing move: Make the representation interface the contribution

VoxelNet connects raw sparse measurements to a detector through learned voxel features. The motivation identifies both the limitations of handcrafted voxel statistics and the difficulty of applying small-object point networks to a full scene. The contribution is the usable interface between these scales, not a claim that every detector component is new.

## Experimental logic

A handcrafted-feature alternative with a similar detection network establishes a meaningful starting point. Learned voxel features improve on that baseline. Comparing bird’s-eye-view and full 3D localization gives the geometric representation a more specific role than a single detection score would.

## Detail placement

The variable-size voxel encoding and its efficient organization belong in the method because they make the central representation practical. Keep ordinary per-class settings compact. Explain which operations are sparse and which subsequent layers use dense feature volumes.

## Interpretation

An RGB overlay helps readers interpret a prediction but does not mean the method uses camera input. Avoid turning learned feature extraction into a claim of having no design assumptions: voxelization and point sampling still structure the input.
