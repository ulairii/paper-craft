## Writing move: Show how complementary representations communicate

PV-RCNN gives voxels and points distinct strengths, then identifies the obstacle to combining them: directly aggregating many voxel features at every proposal grid is expensive. Keypoints summarize the scene before proposal-specific pooling. The intermediate representation makes the complementarity usable.

## Experimental logic

The proposal comparison explains the voxel stage. Direct pooling versus keypoint aggregation addresses the bridge between representations. Feature-source comparisons distinguish local geometric information from semantic context. These experiments follow the information path established in the introduction.

## Detail placement

Keep the voxel-to-keypoint-to-grid sequence consistent in the overview, method, and results. Explain why a sparse low-resolution volume can leave conventional interpolation with little information. Several radii and feature levels matter through their roles, not through a long list of tensor dimensions.

## Interpretation

Some easy pedestrian and cyclist results do not improve; the paper suggests a limited keypoint budget as a possible explanation. Write the category-specific outcome and label the explanation appropriately. Strong scene-level performance does not establish that every representation benefits every object.
