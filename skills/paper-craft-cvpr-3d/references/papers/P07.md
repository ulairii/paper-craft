## Writing move: Explain the failure that prevents a tempting simplification

3DSSD first shows why removing expensive upsampling is attractive, then why simply deleting it fails: downsampling can remove all points belonging to a small or distant object. Feature-aware sampling and a revised prediction head become the conditions that make the simpler detector possible.

## Experimental logic

Instance survival after sampling measures the proposed failure mechanism. Detection AP then shows why high survival alone is insufficient: retaining background context also supports classification. Table 8 crosses point shifting with assignment rules and shows that center-ness works very differently before and after shifting.

## Detail placement

Explain that sensor returns lie on surfaces, so raw points are poor candidates for a center-based target. This geometric fact earns the candidate-shifting operation its place. Keep the distinction between feature-based selection and the fusion of selection strategies clear.

## Interpretation

The method is point-based but uses voxel sampling to prepare the nuScenes input; avoid an absolute claim that no voxelization occurs anywhere. Runtime comparisons need enough context to explain the operating point, especially where other systems use additional acceleration.
