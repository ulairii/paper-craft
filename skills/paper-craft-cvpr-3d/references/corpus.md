# CVPR 3D perception: source papers

Ten accepted CVPR papers about point-cloud representations, 3D detection, geometric operators, pretraining, and scalable backbones. This specialist focuses on point-cloud perception; it is not a dedicated guide to reconstruction, rendering, or camera-only 3D estimation.

All ten main texts were read, together with all four pages of the Point Transformer V3 supplement. Other supplements are not the basis of these notes. Figure discussions use extracted text and captions, not an assessment of visual styling. These are interpretations of persuasive arguments, not evidence of which writing decisions caused acceptance.
