# NeurIPS reinforcement learning: source papers

Ten accepted NeurIPS papers on sparse rewards, human preferences, model-based control, offline learning, sequence-based decisions, and empirical evaluation.

All ten main texts were read, with relevant supplementary reading recorded in the metadata. Figure discussion uses text and captions. These notes interpret published writing craft; they do not establish what caused acceptance.
