## Writing move: Explain an unfamiliar operation through an ordinary failed attempt

Hindsight Experience Replay moves from a missed hockey shot to learning from the goal actually achieved. The example makes reward relabeling intuitive before the formal algorithm appears. A bit-flipping task then isolates the sparse-reward obstacle before robot manipulation demonstrates its practical relevance. The contribution is a useful change to replay, with off-policy learning and goal conditioning credited as its foundation.

## Experimental logic

Comparisons with ordinary replay establish the contribution, while alternative goals and replay ratios explain its operation. Multi-goal training can help a fixed target, which separates the benefit of training diversity from the evaluation task. The physical robot demonstrates a possible application; the improved trial follows additional simulation training with observation noise.

## Detail placement

Keep the achieved-goal map, reward recomputation, and goal-independent dynamics assumption near the algorithm. Binary rewards still require a success definition. ## Interpretation

The tested shaping rewards fail where hindsight helps; this is not evidence that every shaped reward fails. Too much relabeled replay can hurt by reducing ordinary experience. The small physical demonstration is not a general deployment success rate, and the final policy is not identical to the first simulated policy tested on the robot.
