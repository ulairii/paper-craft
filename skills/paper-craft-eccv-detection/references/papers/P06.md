Hongyu Zhou and Zheng Ge and Songtao Liu and Weixin Mao and Zeming Li and Haiyan Yu and Jian Sun. **ECCV 2022**. [Official paper](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/html/961_ECCV_2022_paper.php) · [Full text](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136690036.pdf).

## Writing move: Show what is lost when a teacher's output is compressed

Dense Teacher questions the intermediate conversion from dense predictions to sparse pseudo-boxes and back to dense supervision. Thresholding, suppression, and reassignment can discard or distort the teacher's information. The proposed representation removes that detour, while region selection explains why preserving every low-confidence response would also be unhelpful.

## Experimental logic

The plain dense-label substitution gives a modest gain; region selection and regression supervision supply further improvements. Suppressing, ignoring, and learning from selected hard negatives makes the information argument concrete. Label-budget results compare methods at different supervision levels, and same-detector baselines distinguish the learning change from the detector family.

## Detail placement

Define continuous teacher targets and the loss that accepts them. State how the top-ranked learning region is selected and what happens outside it. The diagnostic hard-negative experiment uses held-out annotations to identify regions for analysis; the deployed learning rule uses teacher scores. Removing pseudo-box post-processing is not removing detector post-processing at inference.

## Interpretation

Dense does not mean that every prediction should be copied, and a simpler pseudo-label path is not hyperparameter-free. The 1% setting is weaker than one Faster R-CNN comparator but stronger than its same-FCOS baseline. Use that distinction to explain the result rather than forcing an all-settings victory.
