## Experimental logic

Boundary regression, bucketing alone, iterative bucketing, and center bucketing separate the proposed ingredients. Bucketing alone is poor: the residual is essential for precision. A heavier generic head tests whether capacity explains the side-aware feature gain. Proposal-IoU breakdowns and high-overlap AP then connect the design to tighter localization.
