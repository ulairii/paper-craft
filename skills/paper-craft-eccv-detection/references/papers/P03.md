Hongkai Zhang and Hong Chang and Bingpeng Ma and Naiyan Wang and Xilin Chen. **ECCV 2020**. [Official paper](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2020/papers_ECCV/html/2335_ECCV_2020_paper.php) · [Full text](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2020/papers_ECCV/papers/123600256.pdf).

## Writing move: Let a changing training distribution motivate a changing rule

Dynamic R-CNN first shows that proposal quality changes during training while assignment thresholds and regression-loss shape stay fixed. The two proposed adaptations answer that same mismatch for classification and localization. This makes a training-only contribution understandable without inventing a new inference architecture.

## Experimental logic

High-overlap AP directly tests the promised localization quality. Threshold and regression-scale trajectories show the proposed adaptation in operation; fixed loss-parameter alternatives distinguish adaptation from choosing a better constant. Component combinations explain their complementarity, while different backbones and Mask R-CNN demonstrate reuse in the evaluated detector family.

## Detail placement

Define high quality as high box overlap. Explain the statistic that updates each rule, and why changing the Smooth L1 transition changes the contribution of small errors. A concise algorithm is useful here. Routine update intervals can follow the principle, rather than dominate the method opening.

## Interpretation

The gain at high overlap coexists with slightly lower AP50 in several comparisons. Present that as the intended emphasis, not a universal metric win. No added inference computation applies to the training adaptation; the strongest headline configuration also includes separate backbone and testing enhancements.
