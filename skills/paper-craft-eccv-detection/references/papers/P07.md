Gang Li and Xiang Li and Yujie Wang and Yichao Wu and Ding Liang and Shanshan Zhang. **ECCV 2022**. [Official paper](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/html/2783_ECCV_2022_paper.php) · [Full text](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136690449.pdf).

## Writing move: Adapt a borrowed learning recipe to the task's actual targets

PseCo asks what classification-style pseudo labeling and consistency miss in detection. Pseudo labels include uncertain coordinates, while scale changes affect both labels and feature pyramids. Prediction-guided assignment, regression-consistency weighting, and aligned views are organized under those two task-specific concerns.

## Experimental logic

A plain extra-view control tests whether gains come simply from processing another image. Comparisons with other regression treatments address localization reliability, and classification/localization score combinations test assignment. Convergence curves are paired with per-iteration cost: the extra low-resolution view costs more per step but can reduce the time to useful accuracy.

## Detail placement

Explain which proposals are shared between teacher and student and what the consistency score measures. For scale learning, describe the shifted pyramid levels and shared box supervision; do not invent a direct feature-matching loss. Keep the coordinate transformation explicit, since a detector's box coordinates must change with image scale.

## Interpretation

Regression agreement is a quality proxy, not a guarantee that a pseudo box is correct. The method encourages consistency across the constructed scales, not exact invariance to every resize. Report learning efficiency through the tested schedule and time comparison rather than treating fewer iterations as automatically cheaper.
