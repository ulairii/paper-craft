## Reading the sources

The notes cover each main text and the selected appendices listed in its header. P01/P02 use pinned accepted author versions with ICLR publication headers; the other papers use official proceedings PDFs. Source versions and reading coverage are available in [corpus.json](corpus.json).

The collection spans sampling, decomposition, verification, revision, search, and compute allocation. It combines method papers with analyses so authors can learn several ways of presenting a contribution. The writing lessons explain what makes the arguments compelling; they are not claims about reviewers' private acceptance decisions.
