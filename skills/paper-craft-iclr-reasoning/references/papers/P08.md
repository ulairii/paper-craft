## Writing lesson: Two bottlenecks give a complex system a readable structure

The generation/selection split gives readers a map before the search actions and consistency procedure become detailed (Fig. 2, Section 3). The system's parts remain understandable because they have different jobs in reaching the final answer.

**Borrow this move.** Introduce the few functional responsibilities that organize the system. Explain actions by what they enable, then show how outputs pass between parts. Keep one term per role rather than multiplying names for the same object.

**Original writing illustration.** “Search proposes reasoning paths; a second model helps select among them” supplies a useful overview before the mechanics. “Our integrated collaborative architecture comprises multiple synergistic modules” gives no such map.

## Supporting reading notes

**Narrative and paragraphs.** Section 1/Fig. 2 (p. 2) separates generating useful paths from selecting them reliably. Actions in Section 3.2 (pp. 4–5) serve distinct reasoning behaviors. Section 3.3 (p. 6) explains how an auxiliary model continues a partial trajectory and checks consistency. The number of components is not itself a contribution.

**Experimental logic.** Table 1 (p. 5) adds actions incrementally. Table 4 (p. 9) crosses generators with selectors. Table 5 (p. 9) compares auxiliary models. Appendix A.1/Table 6 (p. 13) includes a random replacement reward. These controls distinguish effects of the search space, candidate quality, verification information, and additional complexity.

**Interpretation.** No supervised fine-tuning does not mean no auxiliary model or no additional cost. Phi-3 Mini 3.8B serves as the auxiliary model. Standalone task performance in Table 2 (p. 7) shows why fewer parameters need not mean weaker task capability. Describe parameter sizes and training requirements explicitly rather than automatically inheriting a narrative of no help from stronger models.

**Detail placement.** Appendix A.3/Table 10 (p. 14) reports that 32 rollouts entail approximately 149–167 calls and 349k–367k tokens across its two models. A rollout is not one complete independent sample, so comparing it with 32 ordinary samples does not establish equal cost. Explain rollout expansion, auxiliary calls, and token accounting in the main text; full action prompts can go in the appendix.

**Transferable recommendation.** Fix candidates when claiming a better selector, and fix the selector when claiming better search. When claiming cooperation among weak models, report both parameter counts and standalone task capability rather than substituting size for capability.
