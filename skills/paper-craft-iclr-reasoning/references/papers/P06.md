## SCoRe: derive two-stage training from behavioral diagnoses

**Narrative and paragraphs.** Section 3 defines first/second attempts and self-correction metrics. Section 4/Figs. 3–5 (pp. 5–6) diagnoses distribution shift in offline errors and behavioral collapse before Section 5 introduces the training method. Components answer previously demonstrated problems rather than receiving post hoc explanations. Appendix Fig. 11 (p. 19) explicitly diagrams the design logic.

**Experimental logic.** Stage I constrains first-attempt drift and improves the second attempt. Stage II jointly optimizes both attempts with progress-based reward shaping. Table 4 (p. 10) removes Stage I, reward shaping, and online first-attempt samples individually while reporting first-attempt accuracy, second-attempt accuracy, and their difference. Final accuracy alone cannot establish that the model learned to revise.

**Interpretation.** Table 2 (p. 9) reports 52.6→41.4 for the base model, a self-correction delta of −11.2, and 60.0→64.4 for SCoRe, a delta of +4.4. The delta improves by 15.6 percentage points; that is not a 15.6-point gain from one SCoRe revision. Incorrect-to-correct 5.8% and correct-to-incorrect 1.4% use all problems as the denominator, not conditional correction rates. Correctness rewards during training and the absence of oracle feedback at test time (p. 8) must be stated separately.

**Detail placement.** Explain error sources, reward timing, frozen or constrained quantities, and stopping/selection rules in the main text. Parameter tables and complete neutral revision prompts can go in Appendices B–C (pp. 18–20). Section 6.2 (p. 10) compares 2K parallel responses with K two-turn attempts; matching response counts does not establish equal token costs. The plateau over multiple turns in Appendix A.3 (p. 17) limits claims of indefinite scaling.

**Transferable recommendation.** Framing the contribution as learning useful revisions while avoiding damage requires transition-level evidence. The experiments do not prove that RL is the only viable approach. See worked-example.md for the full analysis.
