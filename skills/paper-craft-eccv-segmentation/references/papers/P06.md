## Writing move: Reformulate variable instances into a predictable output structure

SOLO asks how instances differ before introducing its network. Location and size provide an indexing scheme for instance masks. The dataset observation about center separation makes that reformulation plausible; it does not prove that collisions are impossible. Lead with the output representation, then show how semantic categories and masks share the same grid index.

## Experimental logic

Grid resolution, feature pyramids, and coordinate input test the assumptions behind the representation. Dice loss is compared with other objectives, and the appendix applies it to Mask R-CNN, where it hurts: the useful loss is linked to full-image mask imbalance rather than presented as universally better. An oracle mask replacement exposes remaining mask-quality headroom.

## Detail placement

Keep grid assignment, scale assignment, and positional input in the method because they define how instances are separated. Explain that duplicate predictions still require NMS. Head-depth saturation and nearly equivalent alignment variants can be summarized briefly rather than consuming the central narrative.

## Interpretation

Comparable overall AP to an improved Mask R-CNN supports a simpler alternative even though small-object AP is weaker. The decoupled head reduces output channels from a square to a linear dependence on grid size; its empirical performance does not make its factorization an exact representation of every possible mask collection.
