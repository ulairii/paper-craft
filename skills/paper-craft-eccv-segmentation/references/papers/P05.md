## Writing move: Choose context units that match the label being predicted

OCR motivates a pixel representation through its object class. Instead of attending indiscriminately to individual pixels, it first builds soft semantic regions and then relates pixels to those region representations. Write the three steps in this order so that the reader understands the information being summarized before reading the attention equations.

## Experimental logic

Region-supervision ablations distinguish the structured grouping signal from an ordinary auxiliary loss. Comparisons use strengthened context baselines. The panoptic extension reports that most of the gain comes from stuff segmentation, connecting the transfer result to the component OCR actually changes.

## Detail placement

Explain that regions correspond to semantic classes, including stuff, rather than separate object instances. Disconnected regions can share a class representation. The context-module cost comparison excludes the backbone; that scope belongs beside the efficiency result, while routine training details belong in setup.

## Interpretation

Ground-truth regions and relations form an oracle diagnostic, not a deployable result. The strongest Cityscapes submission also uses a stronger backbone, extra pretraining, and SegFix; its entire gain cannot be attributed to OCR. Use the module comparisons to explain the architectural contribution and the full system to show achievable performance.
