## Writing move: Move instance identity from the cropped input into the prediction function

CondInst uses a concrete ambiguity: two similarly looking people require different foreground masks. It contrasts a shared mask head applied to instance crops with an instance-conditioned head applied to shared features. This is a strong way to explain dynamic weights without beginning with a controller architecture.

## Experimental logic

Relative-coordinate and appearance ablations ask where instance information comes from. Mask-head depth and width comparisons show that a small nonlinear head suffices; increasing capacity is not the contribution. Box versus mask NMS tests whether ROI independence genuinely permits removing the box branch.

## Detail placement

Distinguish the 169 generated parameters of each mask head from the full model's parameters. Keep relative coordinates, shared mask features, and the training/inference role of boxes explicit. The default implementation still predicts boxes for fast NMS, even though masks do not require ROI crops.

## Interpretation

Against the improved Detectron2 Mask R-CNN baseline, the paper reports comparable speed with slightly better AP; the larger speed gain uses the original baseline. Upsampling helps, but the larger factor is not the most accurate in the ablation. Explain the tested tradeoff without claiming that every resolution increase improves detail.
