## Writing move: Make complementary operations answer one segmentation problem

DeepLabv3+ connects two familiar strengths: an encoder gathers multi-scale context, while a decoder restores spatial detail. This gives the combination a reason beyond adding modules. Introduce the information lost during encoding before describing how low-level features recover it.

## Experimental logic

The decoder comparison earns a small design: reducing low-level channels and using two refinement convolutions is enough; deeper or progressively fused alternatives do not improve the result. The trimap evaluation then measures the boundary region named in the motivation, giving the overall mIoU result a specific explanation.

## Detail placement

Keep the channel reduction in the method because it prevents low-level features from overwhelming semantic features. Separate decoder gains from backbone, pretraining, output stride, and test-time augmentation in the results. Put the ordinary optimizer recipe in setup.

## Interpretation

The boundary experiment supports better boundary predictions in the tested setting. It does not establish perfect shape recovery. Image-level pooling helps on VOC but can hurt on Cityscapes, so context is a design choice with dataset-dependent effects. Report MAC reductions as computation reductions, not measured latency.
