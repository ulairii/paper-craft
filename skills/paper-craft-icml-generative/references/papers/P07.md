# P07: TabDDPM: Modelling Tabular Data with Diffusion Models

Kotelnikov, Akim; Baranchuk, Dmitry; Rubachev, Ivan; Babenko, Artem. **ICML 2023**. [Official paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/kotelnikov23a.html) · [Full text](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/kotelnikov23a/kotelnikov23a.pdf).

Source locations: Section 4 and Figure 1; Sections 5.1–5.3; Tables 3–7; Figures 2–4; Limitations and discussion; appendices A–D.

## Writing move: Make a new-domain application answer a domain-specific question

TabDDPM makes the adaptation concrete through heterogeneous features: numerical and categorical columns require different corruption processes, while a shared denoiser models their relationships. The paper then asks whether the resulting synthetic data is useful to downstream learners. The contribution is more specific than importing a fashionable model into another domain.

## Experimental logic

Feature distributions and dependence measurements explain modeling behavior; training on synthetic data and testing on real data measures practical utility. Comparing weak default learners with tuned learners reveals how an evaluation protocol can create an exaggerated impression that synthetic data surpasses real data. A strong interpolation baseline then forces a more informative discussion of why a deep generator may still be useful.

## Detail placement

State the treatment of feature types, class conditioning, and regression targets in the method. Explain what downstream utility measures before presenting its table. Detailed search spaces can stay in the appendix. The MLP evaluation adds evidence that the synthetic data is useful beyond the learner used for tuning.

## Interpretation

Nearest-record distances and a black-box attack measure particular disclosure risks; they do not establish differential privacy or legal permission to release data. The paper's limitations make this explicit. Table 6 also contains an exception to the broad attack-resistance trend. Sell useful synthetic data and the evaluated tradeoff, rather than automatic privacy from synthesis.
