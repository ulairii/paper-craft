## Writing move: Separate two learning problems to remove a data bottleneck

AudioLDM's central move is to separate learning cross-modal correspondence from learning audio synthesis. A pretrained aligned space allows audio embeddings to condition the generator during training and text embeddings to condition it during use. This explains why the diffusion stage can use audio without paired captions, and makes the adaptation more than a change of modality.

## Experimental logic

The conditioning comparison tests audio embeddings against text embeddings with augmentation in both settings. Compression experiments examine a quality–cost tradeoff. Human judgments and automatic metrics give different views of conditioning augmentation, while zero-shot manipulation demonstrates reuse of the trained generator. These comparisons develop the specific interface proposed by the method.

## Detail placement

Distinguish the CLAP conditioning space from the VAE space in which diffusion generates samples. The audio-only claim applies to diffusion training: CLAP already uses paired data. Likewise, single-GPU diffusion training does not describe every pretrained component's cost. Keep these boundaries in the setup, with full component training details in supporting material.

## Interpretation

The best full-data score includes a broader corpus and adaptation to AudioCaps. Some automatic metrics prefer different guidance settings, and augmentation improves subjective scores while worsening several objective ones. The zero-shot super-resolution result does not beat the strongest specialized baseline. Describe the useful reuse and measured quality clearly without claiming every component is aligned perfectly or every metric improves.
