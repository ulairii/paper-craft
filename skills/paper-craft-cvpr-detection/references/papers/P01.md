## Writing move: Transfer a strong representation through two concrete obstacles

The introduction turns stalled detection progress into a specific transfer question: can classification CNNs help detection? Localization and limited detection labels become the two obstacles. Region proposals and pretraining followed by fine-tuning then read as answers, rather than an arbitrary pipeline. Figure 1 teaches the four operations before their implementation.

## Experimental logic

Layer-by-layer comparisons with and without fine-tuning explain where generic features remain useful and where adaptation matters. The error diagnosis then motivates bounding-box regression. This gives the analysis a consequence: a measured weakness leads to a small method improvement.

## Detail placement

Keep the region-to-feature interface and the reason for transfer learning in the main text. Cite the inherited CNN architecture instead of retelling every layer. Move alternative warping schemes and routine configuration away from the central explanation.

## Interpretation

The error breakdown supports a localization diagnosis; the segmentation extension supports reuse of region features. Neither needs to become a claim that CNNs solve every vision task. Borrow the concrete transfer argument, not the historical biological preamble or colorful language about removing layers.
