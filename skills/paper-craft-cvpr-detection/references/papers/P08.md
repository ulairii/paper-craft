## Writing move: Explain why the simple replacement fails before completing it

The dense, dense-to-sparse, and sparse comparison makes the proposed starting representation recognizable. Learnable boxes alone provide too little information, which motivates proposal features and their role in the dynamic head. This is more persuasive than introducing three named components before explaining what each contributes.

## Experimental logic

The progression from learnable proposals to iterative refinement and dynamic interaction reconstructs the method’s logic. A weak naive replacement is useful evidence: it teaches why the complete design is needed. Proposal-count and stage-count results then describe the useful operating range.

## Detail placement

The dynamic-head pseudocode earns main-text space because the generated parameters are otherwise difficult to follow. Keep proposal boxes and proposal features distinct. Initialization sweeps can be compact; they support the simplicity of the starting representation but need not become the central story.

## Interpretation

Traces of boxes through refinement illustrate the claimed iterative behavior. Sparse proposals and sparse interaction do not imply that every operation in the image backbone is sparse. Use the precise object of the adjective rather than adding repeated qualifications everywhere.
