## Writing move: Turn a representation tradeoff into the organizing idea

The opening makes the tension concrete: fine-resolution features have weak semantics, while strong semantic features have coarse resolution. Top-down and lateral connections become a direct response. Figure 2 distinguishes the purpose of the proposed pyramid from a superficially similar top-down architecture by showing where predictions are made.

## Experimental logic

The ablations ask whether top-down enrichment, lateral connections, and multiple prediction levels matter. These questions return to the semantic-versus-resolution tension from the introduction. Fixed-proposal detector experiments and complete detector experiments explain two different uses of the same representation.

## Detail placement

Define bottom-up and pyramid features consistently, and explain the fusion block once. The paper explicitly sets aside more complex connections that offered only marginal benefit. Such variants need not become a major section merely because they required implementation effort.

## Interpretation

The mask-proposal extension strengthens the reusable-representation story. Small-object performance connects naturally to the resolution argument. Explain that connection without treating any individual improvement as a complete causal account of every gain.

Before: Our novel architecture exploits rich hierarchical synergistic information.
