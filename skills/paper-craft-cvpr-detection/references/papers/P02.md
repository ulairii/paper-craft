## Writing move: Make each promised benefit return as an experiment

The single-regression framing makes speed, global image context, and transfer to artwork understandable consequences of the design. The introduction also acknowledges localization errors. This allows the paper to offer an attractive operating point without pretending to dominate every accuracy comparison.

## Experimental logic

The experiment sequence follows the advertised benefits: real-time performance, error types, complementarity with Fast R-CNN, and artwork generalization. The error analysis is especially productive: fewer background errors motivates combining the two detectors, so the next experiment follows from the previous finding.

## Detail placement

Explain the output grid and box prediction before listing network layers. Put grid crowding and localization limitations near the method because they explain later errors. Keep the ensemble separate from the real-time model when telling the speed story.

## Interpretation

Use an error profile to explain what changes, not just how much average precision improves. Artwork experiments illustrate a particular distribution shift. A successful transfer example is more informative when its domain is named than when it becomes a vague claim of robustness.

## Original writing illustration

Before: Our detector achieves astounding effectiveness and generalization.

After: The detector trades some localization accuracy for real-time prediction and makes fewer background errors than the comparison detector.
