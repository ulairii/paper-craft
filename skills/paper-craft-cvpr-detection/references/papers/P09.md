## Writing move: Use one conceptual vocabulary across the whole paper

Scale, spatial position, and task define the opening challenges, the related-work categories, and the three attention operations. The level-by-space-by-channel tensor turns these challenges into a common representation. This repeated vocabulary gives unification a concrete meaning.

## Experimental logic

The component combinations test the three operations, while feature analyses illustrate their behavior. Integration into several detector families answers a different question: whether the common representation is useful across existing interfaces. The paper thus gives both internal and external substance to its unifying claim.

## Detail placement

Introduce the meaning of each tensor dimension before its formula. Explain the full-attention cost before factorization. A detector-integration diagram is useful because interfaces vary; it prevents a claim of generality from remaining an abstract slogan.

## Interpretation

The strongest component gain can guide the discussion without proving a universal ranking of attention mechanisms. The block-count plateau is informative, not embarrassing. Borrow the coherence of the taxonomy rather than repeatedly writing novel, unified, and effective.
