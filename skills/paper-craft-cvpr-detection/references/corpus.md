# CVPR object detection: source papers

Ten accepted CVPR papers selected for complementary writing structures: representation transfer, real-time tradeoffs, multiscale features, localization quality, assignment diagnosis, scalable efficiency, sparse proposals, unified heads, and training convergence. This is a craft collection, not a claim that these are the ten best detectors or an exhaustive literature review.

All ten main texts were read. Notes identify the sections, tables, and figures used; DN-DETR’s two-page supplement was also read. Other supplements are not the basis of these notes. Figures were studied through their descriptions and captions in the extracted text; these notes do not analyze visual styling. Acceptance establishes the publication venue, not the causal effect of a writing choice.
