# CVPR segmentation: source papers

Ten accepted CVPR papers covering dense prediction, context, refinement, attention, boundaries, and task unification. Chosen for complementary explanatory structures, not as a ranking of all segmentation work.

All ten main texts were read. OneFormer’s supplementary sections A–F and figure captions were also read; other supplements are not the basis of these notes. Figure discussions use the extracted descriptions and captions and do not assess visual styling. The lessons interpret why an argument is persuasive; acceptance does not establish which writing choices caused acceptance.
