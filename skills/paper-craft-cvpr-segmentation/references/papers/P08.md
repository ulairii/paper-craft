## Writing move: Specify what a universal architecture must accomplish

Mask2Former starts with duplicated architecture development across segmentation tasks, then identifies the obstacle to a common design: earlier universal architectures underperform specialized ones and can be difficult to train. Localized masked attention and efficient high-resolution processing answer these obstacles.

## Experimental logic

Component comparisons report instance, panoptic, and semantic results together. This makes universality part of the experimental argument rather than an adjective supported by one benchmark. Pixel-decoder comparisons are particularly informative because a design that helps one task is not always best for another.

## Detail placement

Explain the mask-to-attention connection and the feature-scale schedule. The diagram caption says which auxiliary elements are omitted for readability, a useful way to keep an overview legible. Point-based loss calculation matters because memory accessibility is an explicit part of the motivation.

## Interpretation

The limitations section distinguishes one reusable architecture from a single model trained once for all tasks. Small-object results also temper the high-resolution story. Do not borrow the numerical claim of reduced research effort as though it were a measured labor study.
