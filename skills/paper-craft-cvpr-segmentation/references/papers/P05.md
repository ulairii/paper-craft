## Writing move: Give two attention operations different explanatory roles

DANet frames the problem as inconsistent features for pixels that share a label but differ in appearance or location. Position attention relates distant locations, while channel attention relates feature maps. These two axes give the dual design a meaning beyond adding two attention modules.

## Experimental logic

Table 1 compares the two operations individually and together. Figures 4–5 describe corresponding output changes. Figure 6 then examines selected point-attention maps and attended channels, making the long-range association argument more tangible than an aggregate score alone.

## Detail placement

Define what each attention matrix indexes before presenting the products. Explain why the outputs are fused in parallel; the paper gives memory as a practical reason for avoiding a cascade. Keep separate the architecture comparison and the additional augmentation and inference improvements in Table 2.

## Interpretation

The feature visualizations illustrate learned associations; they do not establish that every channel has one fixed class meaning. The paper reports competitive VOC performance alongside stronger results elsewhere. A clear multi-dataset account need not claim the best score in every table.
