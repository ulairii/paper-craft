## Writing move: Reinterpret an existing model before adding complexity

FCN starts from the possibility of turning classification networks into dense predictors. The fully convolutional reinterpretation establishes the core capability; the tension between recognizing what is present and locating where it is then motivates skip fusion. This ordering lets a simple foundation carry a substantial contribution.

## Experimental logic

Converted classification backbones establish that transfer works. Table 2 and Figure 4 then follow the progression from coarse to finer predictions. The improvement diminishes at the finest stage, which gives a reason to stop adding skips. Figure 5 compares image-wise training with sampled losses and explains why a simpler training approach remains effective.

## Detail placement

Explain the convolutional reinterpretation and how coarse predictions return to pixels. The mathematical equivalences in §3 teach the design space, but a new paper using an ordinary FCN need not repeat them. The multimodal and two-output extensions in §5 show what the common representation makes possible.

## Interpretation

The discussion connects small metric changes with visible detail, while Figure 6 also names a failure involving lifejackets. Mean overlap and fine boundary quality need not improve proportionally. Borrow the what-and-where distinction, not ornamental terms for the feature hierarchy.
