## Writing move: Use one design principle to connect several blocks

RefineNet explains why coarse predictions lose detail and why maintaining high resolution throughout a deep network is expensive. Reusing earlier features answers the first issue, while the refinement path preserves the benefit of downsampling. Residual connections provide a shared rationale for otherwise separate components.

## Experimental logic

Table 2 studies context pooling, backbone depth, and multiscale evaluation on two tasks. Table 9 then compares different arrangements of the refinement blocks. The strongest two-scale arrangement is more expensive, so the standard design remains a single-scale cascade; this is a useful design decision, not a result to hide.

## Detail placement

Show one canonical architecture before its reusable block, then explain how information and gradients pass through it. Put alternative arrangements after the reader understands that common unit. A long dataset list does not require a repeated paragraph declaring superiority for each dataset.

## Interpretation

The paper notes that CRF refinement gives only a marginal gain on VOC and does not apply it everywhere. Use such observations to explain why a simpler system is sufficient. Do not turn every implemented option into a claimed contribution.
