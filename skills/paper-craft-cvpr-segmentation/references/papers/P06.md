## Writing move: Make a strong baseline a worthwhile contribution

Panoptic FPN states its ambition precisely: a simple shared network for semantic and instance segmentation. It anticipates the apparent compromise between the two tasks and shows that a lightweight semantic branch can reuse an instance-oriented backbone. The paper sells a useful foundation rather than disguising a small extension as architectural reinvention.

## Experimental logic

The experiment roadmap is unusually clear. First establish the semantic branch, since the inherited instance branch is already understood; then study joint training; finally evaluate the joint task. Table 3 asks both whether sharing preserves accuracy at lower cost and whether a larger shared backbone helps at similar cost.

## Detail placement

Keep the two output types and their overlap resolution in the method because these define a panoptic prediction. Explain loss balancing as the means of training the branches together. Compact width and fusion comparisons justify simple defaults without expanding into an exhaustive design search.

## Interpretation

A shared architecture can be valuable even if individual task scores shift slightly. The primary finding is a useful joint solution. The authors explicitly leave orthogonal architectural improvements outside scope; a baseline paper benefits from that focus.
