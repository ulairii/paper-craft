## Writing move: Let a few recognizable errors motivate context

PSPNet gives context a concrete job: a boat resembles a car locally, but its surroundings help disambiguate it. The method section organizes failures into mismatched relationships, confusing categories, and inconspicuous classes before introducing pyramid pooling. The reader understands the missing information before learning the module name.

## Experimental logic

Table 1 contrasts a single global pool with regional pyramid pooling. Separate analyses address the auxiliary loss and deeper backbones, while Table 4 places the training and architecture improvements together. The later return to the original failure examples closes the explanatory loop.

## Detail placement

Keep the regional pooling levels and their fusion visible. Distinguish the auxiliary training branch from the inference path in a short sentence. Crop size and normalization matter to the reported system, but the writing lesson is to explain their effect succinctly rather than make implementation settings the central argument.

## Interpretation

Explain what the surrounding scene helps resolve. Avoid replacing this with a vague assertion that context is important. The paper’s historical use of open vocabulary should not be copied into a modern claim of open-vocabulary recognition on unseen labels.
