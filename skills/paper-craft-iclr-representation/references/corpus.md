# ICLR representation learning: source papers

Ten accepted ICLR papers on information objectives, graph representations, hard negatives, collapse, masked pretraining, and language–image alignment.

All ten main texts and the specified relevant appendices were read. Official conference entries supply bibliographic metadata; the versioned author arXiv PDFs linked in the notes are the actual reading sources. Some versions postdate the conference. They are not asserted to be identical to the conference PDFs. Figure discussions use extracted text and captions, not visual styling. These lessons interpret scientific presentation rather than establish what caused acceptance.
