# P05: VICReg: Variance-Invariance-Covariance Regularization for Self-Supervised Learning

Adrien Bardes; Jean Ponce; Yann LeCun. **ICLR 2022**. [Official paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=xm6YD62D1Ub) · [Full text read: arXiv:2105.04906v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.04906v3).

Source locations: Sections 2, 4–6; Tables 1–5; Appendix D.4–D.8; Appendix E.

## Writing move: Make architectural freedom a contribution that experiments demonstrate

VICReg explains three terms through their jobs: matching views, maintaining variation, and reducing redundant coordinates. It identifies variance preservation as the central addition and credits earlier decorrelation work. Competitive rather than uniformly leading recognition scores are enough for its argument because the main benefit is an explicit, modular objective that can regularize different branches independently.

## Experimental logic

Table 4 distinguishes avoiding constant collapse from obtaining useful representations. Table 5 changes weight sharing and architectures; the image–text experiment changes modalities. Those tests directly support the architectural-freedom claim. The added-regularization experiments also show that combining useful ingredients does not guarantee an additional gain.

## Detail placement

Keep the jobs of the three terms and the distinction between downstream representations and expanded embeddings in the main text. State that normalization is not required by the objective, while the standard configuration still uses hidden-layer batch normalization. Large coefficient and width sweeps can move to the appendix.

## Interpretation

Decorrelated coordinates need not be statistically independent. Batch-size robustness includes learning-rate tuning. The reported repeated-run stability covers the linear evaluation, not every experiment. Appendix E separates timing over 100 epochs from the final accuracy numbers; do not silently treat them as a matched training budget.
