# P04: EnFuzz: Ensemble Fuzzing with Seed Synchronization among Diverse Fuzzers

Yuanliang Chen; Yu Jiang; Fuchen Ma; Jie Liang; Mingzhe Wang; Chijin Zhou; Xun Jiao; Zhuo Su. **USENIX Security 2019**. [Official paper](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity19/presentation/chen-yuanliang) · [Full text](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/sec19-chen-yuanliang.pdf).

Source locations: Sections 1–5; ensemble example, synchronization and diversity comparisons; complete appendix.

## Writing move: Explain cooperation through information exchanged during search

EnFuzz begins with different fuzzers succeeding on different programs. A small search example then makes the central idea concrete: sharing an intermediate seed can let another fuzzer reach behavior it would miss alone. The contribution is cooperation during exploration, not merely collecting the union of final reports. The paper gives familiar components a new relationship.

## Experimental logic

A no-synchronization ensemble with the same members is the natural explanatory comparison. Equal-core experiments establish the value under a resource budget. Alternative ensembles explore complementarity, while the change from QSYM-containing ensembles on small benchmarks to other combinations on larger programs makes the deployment setting relevant.

## Detail placement

Keep the member configuration, core allocation, synchronization mechanism, and seed-sharing rule visible. Distinguish manually selected diversity from learned optimal composition. Put the long program and bug lists in the appendix, retaining one case that shows which member produced the seed and how another used it.

## Interpretation

Changing ensemble members changes more than an abstract diversity score, so a ranking does not establish a universal law of diversity. Some individual fuzzers still win on particular programs or metrics. The benchmark bug total and the separately reported new-bug total refer to different collections. Integration can be valuable without every member or every target improving.
