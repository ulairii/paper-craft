# P04: Glow: Generative Flow with Invertible 1x1 Convolutions

Kingma, Durk P; Dhariwal, Prafulla. **NeurIPS 2018**. [Official paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/hash/d139db6a236200b21cc7f752979132d0-Abstract.html) · [Full text](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/file/d139db6a236200b21cc7f752979132d0-Paper.pdf).

Source locations: Sections 2–5; Table 1; Figures 2–5; supplementary Sections A–D.

## Writing move: Make an architectural contribution legible through one changed operation

Glow gives a flow architecture a clear identity through learned invertible channel mixing. A table aligns each transformation with its inverse and Jacobian, letting readers understand expressiveness and tractability together. Actnorm addresses small per-device batches, while inherited coupling layers supply the surrounding structure. The paper sells a coherent architecture rather than claiming to invent normalizing flows.

## Experimental logic

Likelihood comparisons establish density modeling; permutation and coupling ablations identify the architectural contribution. High-resolution synthesis and latent manipulations show uses that a likelihood table cannot convey. The supplement distinguishes qualitative settings from quantitative ones, and reports the cost of learned mixing. The experiments make the architectural choice concrete instead of treating every new layer as equally important.

## Detail placement

Keep invertibility, the determinant computation, and Actnorm initialization in the method. State the bit depth and sampling temperature where they affect the shown result: qualitative and likelihood configurations differ. Attribute-based directions use labels after training, so explain that interface. Full channel counts and long image galleries can follow the main argument.

## Interpretation

Exact density computation does not imply that likelihood and perceived quality always agree. Lower-temperature sampling changes the distribution. The LU parameterization reduces asymptotic determinant cost, but the reported practical speed difference is small. Constant-memory reversibility is a possible implementation advantage; the reported implementation uses checkpointing.
