## Writing move: Turn a crowded literature into independent choices that can be improved

EDM reframes apparently coupled diffusion methods as a set of tangible design choices. A common notation and comparison table make the contribution useful before the new results arrive. The exposition starts with deterministic sampling because it is easier to reason about, then reintroduces stochasticity. Training preconditioning follows as a separate design axis.

## Experimental logic

Fixed pretrained networks isolate sampler changes. A fixed deterministic sampler then supports the training study. These two stages make modularity an experimental claim. Preconditioning alone does not improve every FID; its role is to support a stronger loss design. Re-examining stochasticity after training improvements reveals that a useful choice for one model can become harmful for another.

## Detail placement

Keep the common parameterization, sampler pseudocode, and comparison table in the main text. Explain what one network evaluation means. Put detailed reformulations, complete grids, and numerical-error derivations in the appendix. Report that FID is the minimum of three evaluations and that checkpoints are selected by FID when using those numbers.

## Interpretation

Modularity does not mean every combination performs equally well. The best stochastic settings are model-specific, while deterministic sampling is strongest for the improved CIFAR-10 models. A small unexplained advantage of a less standard solver parameter is deliberately left out of the final method. This is a useful example of preferring an understandable design to an extra knob.
