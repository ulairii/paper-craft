# P08: Diffusion Models Beat GANs on Image Synthesis

Dhariwal, Prafulla; Nichol, Alexander. **NeurIPS 2021**. [Official paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/49ad23d1ec9fa4bd8d77d02681df5cfa-Abstract.html) · [Full text](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/49ad23d1ec9fa4bd8d77d02681df5cfa-Paper.pdf).

Source locations: Sections 1–9; Tables 1–5; Figures 2–4; supplementary Sections A–D and F–H.

## Writing move: Connect two practical gaps to two complementary contributions

Diffusion Models Beat GANs on Image Synthesis motivates two gaps: architecture refinement and control over the fidelity–diversity tradeoff. Architecture improvements and classifier guidance answer those gaps in the same order. The paper credits earlier guidance ideas while making the large-scale application and adjustable sampling behavior central. Upsampling then extends the demonstrated capability.

## Experimental logic

Architecture ablations consider quality and wall-clock time, so the final design need not include every accuracy-improving change. Guidance-scale sweeps report FID, precision, and recall together. Combining guidance with upsampling tests complementarity. The compute appendix separates early checkpoints that cross a baseline from the more expensive final model, giving different cost claims different evidence.

## Detail placement

Keep the noisy classifier, gradient scaling, sampling budget, and cascade stages close to the method and results. A generator evaluation count does not include all classifier work. Put complete derivations and architecture settings in the appendix, but retain the tradeoff curves in the main argument. Distinguish selected opening examples from random-sample evidence.

## Interpretation

Increasing guidance can raise precision while lowering recall and eventually worsening FID. Better benchmark FID is not superiority on every metric or GAN-like sampling latency. Compute comparisons may use estimated cross-hardware conversions, and economical early-stopping results are not the cost of the best final checkpoint. The strongest story is improved synthesis with explicit control over a tradeoff.
