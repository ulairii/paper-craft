## Writing move: Explain a collection of techniques through the distinct outcomes they improve

Improved Techniques for Training GANs organizes practical changes around obstacles in training and evaluation. The techniques have different purposes, and the paper makes those purposes visible. Feature matching encourages useful intermediate statistics; minibatch discrimination exposes similarities among generated examples. The contribution is a productive experimental recipe, not a new convergence theorem.

## Experimental logic

Semi-supervised classification and image synthesis test different benefits. Feature matching is particularly useful for classification, while minibatch discrimination supports sample quality. The strongest generation recipe does not include every proposed technique. Ablations therefore help choose a recipe rather than merely celebrate each component. Human judgments add another perspective, including the effect of feedback and expertise.

## Detail placement

Explain what each technique changes before listing its constants. Keep label use explicit in class-aware synthesis and the K+1 classifier. Describe Inception Score as a practical proxy and identify the sampling protocol. Put complete model and optimizer details after the conceptual roles, rather than allowing a long list of tricks to dominate the introduction.

## Interpretation

A better classifier need not imply better generated samples. Inception Score does not establish human realism or complete distribution coverage, and its reported sampling variation is not variation across training seeds. ImageNet examples show recognizable parts with remaining structural failures. That partial success is a useful result when described concretely.
