# NeurIPS generative models: source papers

Ten accepted NeurIPS papers on adversarial learning, normalizing flows, hierarchical VAEs, diffusion training, guidance, and fast sampling.

All ten main texts were read, with relevant supplementary reading recorded in the metadata. The official supplement archive for P02 contains an unrelated paper and was excluded. Figure discussion uses text and captions. These notes interpret the craft of published papers; they do not establish what caused acceptance.
