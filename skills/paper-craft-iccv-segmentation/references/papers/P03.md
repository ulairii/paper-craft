## Writing move: Assign each prediction branch a clear responsibility

Mask R-CNN separates category recognition from spatial mask prediction. This division makes a simple per-class binary mask head reasonable: the box branch already decides the category. Alignment preserves the spatial information that the mask branch needs.

## Experimental logic

Independent sigmoid masks are compared with competing per-pixel classes; a class-agnostic mask variant further tests the division of labor. RoIWarp versus RoIAlign separates interpolation from removal of coordinate quantization. FCN-versus-MLP heads tests retention of spatial layout.

## Detail placement

State that mask loss is applied to the ground-truth class during training. Distinguish binary foreground prediction from the spatial softmax used in the keypoint extension. Explain alignment before listing head layers.

## Interpretation

A shared framework supports several outputs without guaranteeing that every added task improves every other task: keypoint training can slightly hurt box and mask scores. The paper's simplicity is enabled by specific spatial and loss choices.
