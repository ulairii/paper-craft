## Writing move: Show what the simple baseline already explains

Segmenter establishes a strong linear decoder before introducing its mask transformer. This gives global patch encoding much of the credit and makes the decoder an incremental contribution. Class embeddings and patch embeddings interact to form image-dependent class masks.

## Experimental logic

Model size, patch size, decoder, and fine-tuning dataset size answer different questions. Object-size breakdowns connect smaller patches to finer structures and contextual modeling to large objects. The decoder gain is shown against the linear baseline instead of attributing the entire benchmark gain to it.

## Detail placement

Keep pretraining source and patch size visible. Smaller patches add sequence cost even without adding parameters. Report throughput as throughput under the paper's batching setup, not interactive single-image latency.

## Interpretation

Cityscapes results are competitive rather than best. The qualitative discussion acknowledges sharper CNN boundaries. General panoptic extension is a proposed direction, not an evaluated capability. Avoid claiming that global attention removes every inductive bias.
