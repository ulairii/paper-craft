## Writing move: Use a familiar algorithm to reveal a compact representation

EMANet reframes attention as estimating a small set of bases and reconstructing pixel features from them. Responsibility estimation, basis updates, and reconstruction each have a distinct role. The low-rank representation gives the iteration a purpose beyond invoking EM terminology.

## Experimental logic

Basis maintenance and normalization are compared before the final benchmarks. A matrix of training versus evaluation iteration counts shows that extra inference steps and extra training steps have different effects. Cost comparisons include the surrounding unit, not just one matrix product.

## Detail placement

Distinguish per-image basis refinement from moving-average maintenance of the initialization across batches. Explain why direction-preserving normalization fits the basis interpretation. Use only the EM background needed to understand these operations.

## Interpretation

Compact reconstruction does not by itself prove that all discarded information is noise. More training iterations can reduce accuracy, and evaluation improvements saturate. Borrow the algorithmic explanation without turning standard EM convergence into a guarantee of segmentation quality or a global optimum.
