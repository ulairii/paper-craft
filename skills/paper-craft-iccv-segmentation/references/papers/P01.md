## Writing move: Make two sources of information complementary

The deconvolution paper starts from concrete failures of a fixed receptive field: large objects fragment and small objects disappear. A learned decoder restores spatial structure, while proposal-wise processing handles scale. These are connected responses to the opening problem, not an arbitrary list of modules.

## Experimental logic

The qualitative comparison includes cases where the proposal model wins, where FCN wins, and where their ensemble succeeds. This makes complementary local detail and whole-image context the reason for fusion. The results table separates the decoder, CRF refinement, and ensemble.

## Detail placement

Explain the distinct roles of unpooling switches and learned deconvolution filters. Keep the two-stage crop curriculum because centered objects and misaligned proposals pose different training difficulties. Routine layer specifications need less narrative space.

## Interpretation

The strongest reported result includes an FCN ensemble and CRF. Attribute it to that system. Proposal processing does not remove background noise or make global context unnecessary.
