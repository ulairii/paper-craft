## Writing move: Separate finding a region from naming it

MasQCLIP identifies two open-vocabulary bottlenecks: proposals trained on base categories can miss novel regions, and image-level CLIP representations need adaptation for mask classification. Progressive distillation and query-only tuning answer those different problems.

## Experimental logic

Longer teacher training is compared with progressive student training, exposing that extra iterations alone do not improve novel classes. Tuning variants are evaluated both on the source dataset and on ADE20k, separating adaptation from transfer. Base and novel scores reveal a tradeoff that an overall score can hide.

## Detail placement

Distinguish base–novel splits from cross-dataset evaluation, whose category sets can overlap. Explain that the new mask-token query projections are tuned while CLIP's original feature pathway is preserved. One architecture does not mean one checkpoint for every training regime.

## Interpretation

Novel-mask distillation slightly lowers base accuracy and saturates after two rounds. Frozen values support the transfer rationale but do not guarantee recognition of arbitrary concepts. The paper explicitly limits capability by the pretrained CLIP model and fixed proposal generator.
