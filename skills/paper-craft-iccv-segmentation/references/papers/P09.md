## Writing move: Make the failed baseline the bridge to the method

DeOP lays out coupled one-pass and decoupled multi-pass alternatives, then tries a decoupled one-pass baseline. Its weak performance motivates two precise changes: control patch interaction and pool classification-relevant features instead of treating membership weights as ideal classification weights.

## Experimental logic

The baseline, prompt-adapted baseline, each component, and their combination reveal the sources of improvement. Layer placement and severance strength distinguish useful restriction from destroying context. Query-based and convolutional heatmap decoders test the proposed aggregation mechanism. Supplementary proposal recall tests coverage of seen and unseen regions, while broad severance experiments expose severe failures.

## Detail placement

One-pass refers to the CLIP classification stream; a separate proposal network still runs. Patch severance itself has no learned parameters, but the whole segmentation method requires training. Retain these distinctions in efficiency prose.

## Interpretation

The best tested severance acts at the last transformer layer; applying it broadly can hurt. Mask similarity supports the classification-bottleneck hypothesis but is not an oracle isolation. Report speed for the actual backbone and comparison instead of repeating a headline multiplier across settings.
