## Writing move: Explain one inference step before unrolling the model

CRF-RNN makes joint learning intelligible by translating one mean-field iteration into familiar neural operations, then repeating it as a recurrent network. The novelty story is that the recognition network can adapt to the structured inference that follows it.

## Experimental logic

The comparison progresses from FCN to disconnected CRF, frozen-FCN training, and joint training. These settings distinguish better post-processing from adaptation of the unary predictor. Training and test iteration counts are varied separately.

## Detail placement

Retain the order of message passing, compatibility transform, unary combination, and normalization. Explain which parameters are learned: Gaussian bandwidths remain fixed. The label-pair examples make compatibility concrete without requiring a lengthy graphical-model tutorial.

## Interpretation

Mean-field inference is approximate. More iterations do not invariably improve training. Coarse COCO annotations and finer VOC boundaries motivate different uses of data, rather than a blanket claim that more labels solve boundary prediction.
