## English — research communities

**paper-craft** is an open-source collection of research writing skills distilled from accepted papers.

Give your assistant a target conference, your method, and existing results. The entry point selects a relevant specialist to help write the story, explain the method, and turn result tables into a readable argument.

The library currently includes **22 specialists across 8 conferences**, with at least **10 same-conference source papers per specialist**. Each collection includes explicit references, individual reading notes, and concrete writing examples. Coverage spans AI, computer vision, and security.

The focus is writing craft: what belongs in the opening, how a design follows from an observation, what an experiment teaches, and how to say it in plain English.

Here is a [before/after abstract illustration](https://github.com/ulairii/paper-craft/blob/main/examples/abstract-before-after.md), using the same fictional results in both versions. You can also try the two prompts on your own work.

[Explore the project and install](https://github.com/ulairii/paper-craft). If it helps your writing, consider giving it a star. Feedback on topic matching and the clarity of the resulting drafts is welcome.

## Chinese — research communities

**paper-craft** 是一个开源科研写作技能库：从已录用论文中蒸馏写作方法，做成按会议和细分方向组织的 skills。

把目标会议、方法和现有实验结果交给助手，它会选择适配的 skill，帮助组织故事、解释方法，以及把实验表格写成有逻辑的结果分析。

目前有 **22 个方向、覆盖 8 个会议**，每个方向至少参考 **10 篇同会议论文**。项目里列出了论文引用、逐篇阅读笔记和具体写作示例，覆盖 AI、计算机视觉和安全。

重点是写作本身：开头该讲什么，设计如何从观察中自然引出，实验说明了什么，以及怎样用正常、准确的英文表达。

项目提供了[同一组结果的 abstract 前后对比](https://github.com/ulairii/paper-craft/blob/main/examples/abstract-before-after.md)。这是用虚构结果编写的教学示例，不是模型跑分；也附了 prompt，可以拿自己的材料试。

项目与安装方式：[paper-craft](https://github.com/ulairii/paper-craft)。如果对你有帮助，欢迎点个 star。也欢迎反馈：方向有没有选对，写出来的稿子有没有把核心想法讲清楚。
